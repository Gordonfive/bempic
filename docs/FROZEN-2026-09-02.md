# BEMPIC development freeze — 2026-09-02

## Status

BEMPIC development is **halted/frozen** at its current 0.1 research/release-candidate state.

This is a deliberate program decision, not a claim that BEMPIC is complete, released, conformant, or abandoned permanently.

The exact state at the transition is preserved on `archive/v0.1-generation`.

## Reason

OceanMail discovered that Rhizomatica's HERMES ecosystem already provides a proven store-and-forward email baseline over constrained HF links using UUCP/uucpd/uuxcomp together with Mercury, VARA, and ARDOP, plus related radio-control, RaptorQ broadcast, and test-harness work.

OceanMail 0.2 therefore removes BEMPIC from its mandatory implementation path and will first establish a real HERMES-derived maritime email baseline.

Continuing BEMPIC toward a stable wire release before measuring it against that baseline would risk building a second protocol without demonstrated benefit.

## Freeze rules

While frozen:

- do not select or stabilize a public BEMPIC wire codec;
- do not claim a v0.1.0 release or conformance status;
- do not continue feature development merely to complete the existing roadmap;
- do not change BEMPIC to chase HERMES compatibility speculatively;
- preserve specifications, vectors, prototypes, measurements, governance, and research as evidence; and
- accept only maintenance/security/documentation corrections that are necessary to preserve the research accurately, unless the freeze is explicitly lifted.

## Condition for resuming development

BEMPIC may resume only after direct comparative testing against the HERMES baseline under the same constrained-link/modem conditions shows material value worth the additional protocol, implementation, security, interoperability, and maintenance burden.

Relevant evidence may include:

- substantially lower constrained-link airtime or byte cost;
- materially better completion under interrupted/asymmetric contacts;
- persistent continuation through a different peer or transport after the original contact disappears;
- selective-manifest or directional byte-budget behavior the baseline cannot provide economically; or
- another reproducible capability with clear maritime value.

No fixed percentage is mandated in advance. The decision must be measurement-driven.

## Relationship to OceanMail

OceanMail 0.2 is now HERMES-derived/upstream-first. See the OceanMail repository's ADR 0007 (`docs/adr/0007-v0.2-hermes-transition.md`) for the accepted program decision.

BEMPIC remains service-neutral research. If development resumes later, it must still justify itself independently rather than exist only because OceanMail once planned to depend on it.
