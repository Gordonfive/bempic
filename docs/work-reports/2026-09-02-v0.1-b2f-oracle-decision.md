## Assignment scope and decision

This assignment resolves the specification-owned legal and technical definition
of the B2F/LZHUF comparison used by the v0.1 compactness gates. It audits primary
protocol, implementation, release, and license sources; defines an exact
comparison profile; records candidate use boundaries; aligns normative and
machine-readable artifacts; and adds drift-resistant Python and independent
Node validation.

**Decision:** `blocked-no-qualified-oracle`. No implementation is selected and
no B2F result is claimed. None of the candidates currently combines exact
pinned ARSFI compression behavior, a deterministic standalone interface, the
specified complete 250-octet-block B2F envelope, a redistributable prescribed
corpus, independent byte-for-byte reproduction, immutable executable/build
provenance, and a complete mode-specific licensing record. The existing median
reduction threshold of at least 10 percent and per-fixture increase limit of at
most 5 percent remain unchanged and blocked.

No source, fixture, lookup table, or substantial third-party text was copied.
No GPL/AGPL component was added, vendored, linked, downloaded, or executed. No
tag, release, or merge was created.

## Exact normative changes and requirement IDs

The new comparison profile is
`bempic-v0.1-b2f-text-single-message-v1`.

- `REQ-B2F-001` fixes a one-message, attachment-free, no-fault fixture scope;
  exact CRLF RFC 5322/MIME constraints; deterministic semantic fields; and the
  byte-exact prepared B2 header order and body termination.
- `REQ-B2F-002` pins FBB `LZHUF_1` behavior to ARSFI commit
  `dbe96569817018e66e0e5f6add40eed12adc9fd7`, including the 2,048-octet ring,
  60-octet look-ahead, threshold 2, initial spaces, 314-symbol adaptive Huffman
  tree, `0x8000` rebuild frequency, position coding, and image layout.
- `REQ-B2F-003` fixes the measured B2F envelope from the first FC proposal octet
  through the EOT checksum, including FC/F>/FS, binary header, 250-octet data
  blocks, LZHUF prefix/image, EOT/checksum, directions, and every exclusion.
- `REQ-B2F-004` fixes per-fixture fields, exact signed rational calculations,
  the median of per-fixture reductions, the even-count median rule, reproducible
  environment/tool evidence, and the unchanged thresholds.
- `REQ-B2F-005` keeps the oracle and both thresholds blocked until the complete
  reviewed selection package exists.
- `REQ-B2F-006` requires input/output lengths and SHA-256 digests, CRC prefix,
  decoded length, independent decode, and independently generated byte equality.
- `REQ-CONF-004` defines pass/fail evidence and prohibits converting a blocked,
  missing, unavailable, different-envelope, or unresolved-license result into a
  skip, zero, estimate, or pass.
- `REQ-VEC-008` defines the synthetic redistributable content-addressed corpus
  bundle and forbids private, mutable, or unlicensed substitutes.
- `REQ-METRIC-008` now delegates to the exact comparison profile while retaining
  the 10-percent median and 5-percent per-fixture gates.

The conformance matrix now maps every new requirement to concrete artifact,
validator, digest, transcript, calculation, licensing, and independent
reproduction evidence.

## Comparison and legal boundaries

The measured input is a published semantic record with separately published
raw MIME and prepared-B2 byte images. MIME parsing and B2 preparation occur
before measurement. A measured run compresses the exact prepared B2 bytes,
constructs exactly one accepted offset-zero transfer, and counts all
process-visible B2F bytes in both directions. It excludes login/authentication,
session closure, lower-layer framing/FEC/acknowledgements/retransmissions, and
failed or resumed attempts. Batching is forbidden.

The complete LZHUF image is CRC-16/XMODEM in little-endian storage over the
four-octet little-endian uncompressed length plus compressed bitstream,
followed by that length and bitstream. The B2F transcript uses `FC EM`, its
proposal checksum line, `FS +`, the binary subject/offset header, 1–250 byte
data blocks with all non-final blocks exactly 250 bytes, then EOT and the image
checksum. Directional totals must satisfy
`b2f_total_bytes = b2f_send_bytes + b2f_receive_bytes`.

The legal modes remain separate:

- incorporating source requires compatible terms, provenance, notices, and a
  repository decision;
- linking requires compatible terms and complete provenance, and GPL/AGPL
  linking is forbidden by project policy;
- a third-party GPL executable could only be separately obtained and invoked
  as a process, outside this repository and its Apache-2.0 distribution;
- fixture generation still requires redistribution rights, content addressing,
  and independent reproduction; and
- a publication-based implementation must be independently authored without
  copying source, tables, fixtures, or substantial prose.

These are repository conclusions and not legal advice.

## Architecture and repository boundaries

All changes are confined to `Gordonfive/bempic`. No sibling repository was
modified. Candidate projects and official publications were read only. The
decision creates a specification-owned transcript builder/output contract but
does not choose a codec, import an oracle, or assign application semantics.

The new corpus will be synthetic and redistributable. A future oracle package
must choose one of three reviewed routes: an ARSFI-derived permissively licensed
CLI with notices; an unmodified, separately obtained `paclink-unix` process with
no copying/linking/distribution by BEMPIC; or a clean-room executable authored
from publications and independently compared to pinned ARSFI behavior. Each
route still requires an immutable binary digest, hermetic build/runtime record,
two-platform 250-byte transcript reproduction, malformed/decode evidence, and
raw exact-rational result records.
