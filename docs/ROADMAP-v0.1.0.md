# BEMPIC v0.1.0 Roadmap — Frozen

**Status:** development halted/frozen on 2026-09-02; `v0.1.0` was not released or tagged.

The former active release-gate roadmap is no longer an instruction to continue implementation. Its exact pre-freeze contents and all associated evidence are preserved on the `archive/v0.1-generation` branch.

See [FROZEN-2026-09-02.md](FROZEN-2026-09-02.md) for the accepted freeze decision and the conditions required before BEMPIC development may resume.

## Preserved facts

- BEMPIC 0.1 remained a release candidate when development was frozen.
- No stable public wire codec had been approved.
- BEMPIC Reference remained experimental/non-conformant and had open release/conformance gates.
- The B2F comparison, independent-implementation, stable-codec, release-record, and external M4P-review gates were not all complete.
- No `v0.1.0` release or tag should be claimed.

## Resume rule

Do not reactivate this roadmap merely to finish the old checklist.

If BEMPIC development is resumed, create a new roadmap based on direct evidence against the HERMES `UUCP + uuxcomp` baseline under identical constrained-link/modem conditions. The new roadmap must begin with the measured capability that justifies BEMPIC's additional protocol and maintenance cost.
